Visual inspection shows retained coarse room structure at 200k, but repeated wall
speckling and additional depth holes across headings. The largest lost-depth
fraction is at default+90; the largest common-valid depth MAE and >0.1 m error
fraction are at p7_free+270. No predeclared training-quality threshold exists, so
capture success is not relabeled as quality acceptance. The 200k candidate is
not promoted to an equivalent replacement or task default.

Eight views in one apartment do not establish global coverage, safe placement,
temporal consistency or task benefit. Geometry-derived observations can conceal
Gaussian visual loss, and the full reference itself can have invalid depth.

static capture CLI for 250k/300k/full at the same two locations/four offsets: 24
serial processes, one world, 64x48, no PPO construction or updates. Full references
are rerun in the same batch. Budget order alternates by view. Outputs use a fresh
`multiview-refinement-XXXXXXXX` directory; no source assets, drivers or dependency
installation change. This is quality screening before additional timed runs.

creation with stubs and checked all 24 distinct argument combinations; no GPU or
installation was invoked. `git diff --check` passed. No runtime code changed in
this audit/preparation turn. The subsequent 24 desktop captures were audited in
[refinement evidence](p10-multiview-refinement-2026-11-25.md). This does not promote
200k or another cap to a default, nor measure the user's MuJoCo performance fix.
